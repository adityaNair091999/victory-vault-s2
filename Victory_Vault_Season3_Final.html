2 CHAMPIONS LEAGUE — $225

League Phase · GW1–15 — All 30 managers compete simultaneously within a single Head-to-Head (H2H) league structure.

League Phase Tie-Breaker: If managers finish on the same H2H points, overall season FPL points decides who advances.

Knockout Phase · GW15–18 — The Top 8 managers from the H2H table at the conclusion of GW15 advance into a single-elimination bracket. Seeding is based on league-phase ranking: 1st vs 8th, 2nd vs 7th, 3rd vs 6th, 4th vs 5th.

Knockout Tie-Breaker: If a knockout match is tied on gameweek points, the manager with the lower captain score is eliminated. If still tied, the lower vice-captain score decides. If still tied, the manager with fewer overall season FPL points is eliminated.

Bracket tracking, fixtures, and progress hosted on the official Victory Vault website.

3 WORLD CUP TOURNAMENT — $225

Group Stage · GW21–29 — 30 managers divided into 3 head-to-head groups of 10.

Knockout Qualification (8 Managers):

  • Top 2 from each group qualify automatically (6 managers).
  • Two best 3rd-place finishers across all groups qualify on accumulated group-stage points.

Group Stage Tie-Breaker: If H2H league points are tied, overall season FPL points accumulated from GW21 onward decides.

Knockout Phase · GW30–32 — 8 qualifiers enter a single-elimination bracket seeded by cross-group draw:

  • QF1: Group A 1st vs 2nd-best 3rd
  • QF2: Group B 1st vs Best 3rd
  • QF3: Group C 1st vs Group B 2nd
  • QF4: Group A 2nd vs Group C 2nd

Semi-Finals (GW31): QF winners face off. Final (GW32): Winner takes $150, Runner-up takes $75.

Knockout Tie-Breaker: If a knockout match is tied on gameweek points, the manager with the lower captain score is eliminated. If still tied, the lower vice-captain score decides. If still tied, the manager with fewer overall season FPL points is eliminated.
